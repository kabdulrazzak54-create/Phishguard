About PhishGuard

PhishGuard is an educational, defensive cybersecurity project. It analyzes the text of URLs and QR-code contents using transparent, rule-based checks.

Think Before You Click.

Limitations

  • This tool provides an educational risk assessment, not a safety guarantee.
  • It never visits a link, so it cannot see the page content, certificates or redirects.
  • A low score does not mean a link is safe; a high score does not prove it is malicious.
  • The optional threat-database check only covers threats already known to that database.
  • Rules are simple heuristics and can produce false positives and false negatives.

Ethical use

Use PhishGuard to protect yourself and others. Do not use it to build or test phishing campaigns.
